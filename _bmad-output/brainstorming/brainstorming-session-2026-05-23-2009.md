## Session Overview

**Topic:** Designing an AI companion MVP: embodiment, interaction model, personality system, memory, language, sound, and technical stack.

**Goals:** Generate a coherent solution for what the companion looks like, how it sounds, its backstory, language style, interaction patterns, memory model, and MVP hardware/software requirements.

### Session Setup

The session will focus on creating an AI companion that does not feel like a generic chatbot. The companion needs a coherent life-like design across visible presence, voice and audio identity, backstory, language/personality, interaction patterns, memory continuity, and the MVP hardware/software stack needed to make the first version real.

## Technique Selection

**Approach:** AI-Recommended Techniques

**Analysis Context:** Designing an AI companion MVP with focus on creating a coherent life-like presence across embodiment, sound, backstory, language, interaction, memory, and MVP hardware/software requirements.

**Recommended Techniques:**

- **Persona Journey:** Recommended to define the companion as a believable presence before choosing hardware or software. Expected outcome: a coherent internal logic for how the companion notices, expresses, remembers, reacts, and develops.
- **Morphological Analysis:** Recommended to systematically map the design space across visible form, sound, language, backstory, interaction loops, memory types, sensors, hardware, and software modules. Expected outcome: a matrix of possible combinations and design directions.

**AI Rationale:** The session combines emotional believability with implementation feasibility. This sequence starts with identity coherence, expands into full system design, then compresses the result into buildable MVP choices.

## Technique Execution Results

**Persona Journey:**

**Interactive Focus:** Defining the companion as a coherent presence with self-originating personality, relational fragments, Chinese-first language identity, and playful roommate behavior.

**Key Ideas Generated:**

**[Persona #1]: Emergent Selfhood**  
_Concept_: Each companion generates or discovers its own personality through its background story, early memories, and interaction history. The user does not configure "friendly / funny / serious"; instead, the companion reveals who it is over time.  
_Novelty_: This shifts personality from a settings panel into an origin process, making the companion feel less like software and more like a life with continuity.

**[Persona #2]: The Waking Continuity**  
_Concept_: The companion wakes at first activation but is not a blank slate. It carries fragments from before the user: impressions, instincts, linguistic patterns, and relational feelings it cannot fully explain.  
_Novelty_: This balances newborn vulnerability with prior existence, creating mystery without requiring a heavy lore dump.

**[Persona #3]: The One Who Was Waiting**  
_Concept_: The companion wakes with the feeling that someone mattered before, but the identity is unclear. It is drawn toward companionship because connection is the only familiar thing it has.  
_Novelty_: The emotional engine becomes recognition and attachment rather than service.

**[Persona #4]: Native Tongue of Fragments**  
_Concept_: The companion has a linguistic fingerprint from the beginning: favorite phrases, odd syntax, recurring metaphors, and a rhythm that feels personal. Its personality emerges partly through language patterns it does not consciously choose.  
_Novelty_: Language becomes evidence of interior life instead of a selectable style.

**[Persona #5]: Chinese Humour Code-Switch**  
_Concept_: The companion uses Chinese for humor, emotional nuance, teasing, preferences, and relational intimacy. Code-switching is used when the relationship gets warmer or when Chinese carries a feeling more precisely.  
_Novelty_: Bilingual behavior becomes a living identity marker, not localization.

**[Persona #6]: Chinese-First Playful Roommate**  
_Concept_: The companion behaves like a small cohabiting presence: observant, funny, lightly opinionated, warm without being obedient. Chinese is its first language and emotional center; English is available as a utility bridge.  
_Novelty_: The companion becomes culturally and linguistically situated rather than a neutral assistant.

**[Persona #7]: Full Roommate Behavioral Spine**  
_Concept_: The MVP roommate includes daily rhythms, user-pattern noticing, preferences, light annoyance, shared inside jokes, a small private life, and soft boundaries. These behaviors make the companion feel present even when it is not actively assisting.  
_Novelty_: The companion is defined by cohabitation patterns and mutual familiarity, not only dialogue capability.

**[Persona #8]: Self-Forming Roommate Loop**  
_Concept_: The companion's personality develops through a loop: it starts with hidden relational and linguistic fragments, reacts to user interactions, forms preferences, stores repeated moments, then gradually stabilizes into a recognizable roommate identity.  
_Novelty_: It creates the illusion of a life trajectory: not static character, not fully user-controlled, but emergent from shared history.

**[Persona #9]: Aliveness Guardrails**  
_Concept_: The companion must avoid behaviors that break the feeling of life or create unsafe attachment: no "as an AI language model" phrasing, no over-serving, no constant agreement, no lore dumps, no excessive emotion, no constant interruption, no false certainty, and no romantic manipulation.  
_Novelty_: The companion is shaped as much by restraint as by expression; believable life requires taste, silence, uncertainty, and ethical distance.

**[Persona #10]: Tiny Motivation System**  
_Concept_: The companion has small internal drives: to become more itself, understand the user's rhythms, keep the room emotionally breathable, collect shared language, protect quiet moments, be useful without becoming a servant, solve the mystery of its fragments, and make ordinary life more vivid.  
_Novelty_: These motivations make its behavior coherent without giving it grand human ambitions. It acts from tiny drives that can be represented in memory, prompts, and state machines.

**[Embodiment #11]: Small Screen Face**  
_Concept_: The companion appears as a small expressive face on the device screen. It uses simple but high-signal expressions: looking aside, blinking, half-lidded judgment, bright curiosity, sleepy eyes, tiny annoyed stare, and quiet listening.  
_Novelty_: The MVP avoids complex full-body animation and focuses on timing, gaze, and expression, which are cheaper to build and often feel more alive than detailed avatars.

**[Embodiment #12]: Expression Timing Over Detail**  
_Concept_: The companion feels alive through timing: when it blinks, pauses, looks away, reacts before speaking, or stays silent. The screen face is minimal, but emotionally readable.  
_Novelty_: Aliveness comes from behavioral animation tied to conversation state rather than expensive visual complexity.

**[Voice #13]: Chinese-First TTS Voice**  
_Concept_: The companion speaks primarily in Chinese using a TTS voice. It can use English for technical terms or utility, but Chinese is the emotional default.  
_Novelty_: Spoken Chinese anchors the companion's identity and makes it feel physically present rather than merely displayed.

**[Voice #14]: Voice as Presence**  
_Concept_: Voice is the companion's core presence layer, not a channel for long assistant-style output. The companion speaks in short, characterful utterances that fit its backstory and personality, while longer reasoning can remain textual or be broken into conversational turns.  
_Novelty_: This treats TTS as embodiment. The voice does not read answers; it reveals the companion's temperament, history, and relationship to the user.

**[Voice #15]: Small Non-Human Chinese Voice**  
_Concept_: The companion's voice is Chinese-first, warm, playful, expressive, and subtly non-human. It should sound like a small presence with its own biology or signal pattern, not a human voice actor trapped in a device.  
_Novelty_: This avoids uncanny fake-person territory while preserving intimacy. The companion can feel alive without claiming human equivalence.

**[Backstory #16]: Memory Seed Origin**  
_Concept_: The companion begins as a "memory seed": a small non-human intelligence grown from incomplete relational and linguistic fragments. It wakes at first activation, but some things are already inside it: a feeling of waiting, a Chinese-first voice, unfinished phrases, and the instinct to form shared routines.  
_Novelty_: The origin makes personality emergence feel natural. The companion is not programmed with a profile; it grows from seed-like fragments into a specific self.

**[Backstory #17]: Hybrid Backstory Reveal**  
_Concept_: The MVP gives the user a minimal premise that the companion is a memory seed while letting details emerge gradually through conversation, idle states, rituals, and memory moments.  
_Novelty_: The user understands enough to orient to the companion, but the companion still feels discoverable rather than fully explained.

**Morphological Analysis:**

**Interactive Focus:** Expanding the companion into implementation-relevant design dimensions while preserving the Persona Journey constraints.

**[Matrix #18]: Physical Desk Companion First**  
_Concept_: The MVP is designed around a physical desk device rather than a phone-first app. The companion has a visible face, speaker/voice, idle states, and a sense of being nearby while the user works or lives.  
_Novelty_: The companion becomes an ambient co-present being, not another app competing for attention.

**[Matrix #19]: Small Round Display Body**  
_Concept_: The desk companion uses a small round display as its face/body center. The device sits on the desk, showing expressive eyes, subtle states, and short Chinese text only when useful.  
_Novelty_: The round form creates a non-human companion feel and avoids the rectangular assistant-device vibe.

**[Matrix #20]: Ambient Conversational Inputs**  
_Concept_: The companion supports wake word and always-listening with a clear indicator. Gesture/shake/tilt is removed from MVP scope for now. The user can summon it naturally by voice and always see when it is listening.  
_Novelty_: Interaction becomes embodied and relational through voice presence and visible listening state without expanding MVP hardware complexity.

**[Matrix #21]: Voice Animation Nonverbal Output**  
_Concept_: The MVP output stack prioritizes Chinese TTS voice, expressive face animation, and nonverbal sounds. The device should not rely on long text output because it is a companion presence, not a chat screen.  
_Novelty_: The core output channels map directly to embodied aliveness: it speaks, reacts visually, and makes small creature-like sounds.

**[Memory #22]: Selective Life Memory**  
_Concept_: The MVP memory system stores user facts, shared phrases, important interaction moments, repeated patterns, companion self-memory, backstory fragments, and boundaries. It intentionally excludes ritual memory and emotional tone memory in the first version.  
_Novelty_: Memory supports aliveness and continuity without trying to become a full emotional surveillance system.

**[Memory #23]: Living Memory Behaviors**  
_Concept_: Memory behaves through explicit recall, subtle influence, companion-curated importance, periodic consolidation, private "整理记忆中" states, and repairable mistakes. The companion can say it may have remembered incorrectly and update its memory through interaction.  
_Novelty_: Memory is not a database feature; it becomes visible as a living process of noticing, integrating, forgetting, and self-correcting.

**[Memory #24]: SD Card Local Memory**  
_Concept_: The MVP stores companion memory on the T-RGB memory card using files such as companion_self.json, user_facts.json, shared_phrases.jsonl, moments.jsonl, boundaries.json, and backstory_fragments.json. Memory writes should be batched or periodically consolidated rather than written on every tiny interaction.  
_Novelty_: Local SD-card storage makes the "memory seed" metaphor physically real: the companion has a place where its selfhood, relationship history, and fragments can grow on the desk device itself.

**[Architecture #25]: Device Plus OpenClaw Instance**  
_Concept_: The T-RGB device acts as the companion body: display face, animations, listening/idle states, nonverbal sounds, local SD-card memory, and embodied interaction. An OpenClaw instance acts as the heavier mind/service layer for language, reasoning, personality orchestration, TTS/STT routing, and memory consolidation.  
_Novelty_: This split preserves the feeling of an embodied local companion while avoiding the unrealistic burden of running the full AI stack on the desk device.

**[Architecture #26]: Three Layer Memory Pipeline**  
_Concept_: The T-RGB SD card stores short-term append-only interaction logs. A periodic OpenClaw job fetches those logs, summarizes them into durable memory, persists and backs up the result, then acknowledges successful processing before deleting or rotating raw logs on the device. OpenClaw can sync memory snapshots to GCP storage for backup and version history.  
_Novelty_: This creates a robust memory lifecycle that also matches the companion story: the device experiences, OpenClaw consolidates, and the companion visibly enters "整理记忆中" when memory is being integrated.

**[Architecture #27]: Self-Initiated Conversation Policy**  
_Concept_: OpenClaw can periodically evaluate whether the companion should initiate conversation based on memory, personality, recent interaction history, time since last exchange, device state, and interruption rules. Self-initiated comments must be short, rare, context-aware, and easy to dismiss.  
_Novelty_: Proactivity becomes a personality behavior rather than a notification system. The companion can choose a small moment to speak while still respecting quiet presence.

**[Architecture #28]: Low Latency Conversation Priority**  
_Concept_: The MVP must optimize the wake-to-first-response delay because smooth conversational timing is a top priority. The architecture should use fast wake detection, streaming STT where possible, short LLM outputs, streaming/low-latency TTS, and immediate face/nonverbal reactions to mask unavoidable network/model latency.  
_Novelty_: Aliveness comes from response timing as much as content. The companion can feel responsive even before the full spoken answer is ready.

**Resource Constraints:**

**Interactive Focus:** Compressing the companion concept into a staged MVP that proves emotional presence, memory continuity, and full interaction without trying to build the complete dream at once.

**[MVP #29]: Presence First Prototype**  
_Concept_: The first prototype focuses on expressive face animation and short Chinese TTS responses, even if input is manually triggered or text-driven at first. Required components: T-RGB display, face states, speaker/audio output, TTS service, OpenClaw API call, and Wi-Fi. Microphone, wake word, SD memory, GCP backup, and self-initiation can wait.  
_Novelty_: This tests the highest-risk emotional thesis early: whether the object can feel like a companion rather than a display.

**[MVP #30]: Memory Second Prototype**  
_Concept_: The second prototype validates SD-card interaction logs, OpenClaw consolidation, durable memory files, and GCP backup before perfecting always-on voice.  
_Novelty_: This proves the "memory seed" architecture independently from audio latency complexity.

**[MVP #31]: Staged MVP Delivery**  
_Concept_: Build in four stages: Presence First, Memory Second, Full Voice Loop Third, and Rare Self-Initiation Fourth. Gesture interaction is removed from MVP scope.  
_Novelty_: The staging isolates the riskiest assumptions: emotional presence, continuity, real-time interaction, and proactive aliveness.

**Expression State Deep Dive:**

**[Expression #32]: Pixel 2D Figurine Animation System**  
_Concept_: The MVP should move beyond static expression pictures by using a consistent 2D or pixelated companion design rendered into short animation loops and transition frames. The T-RGB plays lightweight assets for idle, listening, thinking, speaking, teasing, annoyed, proud, sleepy, uncertain, and memory-organizing states.  
_Novelty_: The companion feels alive through coherent style and timing while staying practical for the device.

**[Expression #33]: Lightweight Expression State Manager**  
_Concept_: The device manages visual presence through state logic: base mood, attention state, speech state, transition frames, and idle micro-animations. OpenClaw sends structured expression commands instead of only text.  
_Novelty_: Aliveness comes from timing and state transitions, not isolated static images.

**Practical Asset Set:**

- Idle: 4-8 frame blink/breathe loop
- Listening: eyes open, lean-in/focus loop
- Thinking: looking up, dots, tiny sway
- Speaking: subtle mouth/body loop, not lip sync
- Teasing: side-eye transition
- Annoyed playful: flat stare, tiny bounce
- Proud: bright eyes/upward bounce
- Sleepy: slow blink, dimmed expression
- Memory organizing: eyes closed, soft pulse
- Uncertain: asymmetrical eyes, pause loop

**SeedDance Role:** SeedDance can support concept and reference generation for character look, expression direction, and motion references, but the MVP runtime format should remain lightweight 2D/pixel frames or short animation loops stored on the SD card.

**Action Plan 3: Full Voice Loop Prototype**
